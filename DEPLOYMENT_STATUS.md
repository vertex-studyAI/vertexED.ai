## Product certification law

A CI pass, source branch, generated artifact, database metadata query, preview/public URL, provider status, or HTTP 200 is **not** exact production certification by itself.

Production/release status requires direct evidence for the intended immutable revision plus the relevant end-to-end journey. External rate limits, missing secrets, inaccessible production targets, absent test identities, or account-level configuration remain blockers; they are not reasons to weaken gates, fabricate deployment state, expose credentials, or incur unapproved spending.
