## Product certification law

A CI pass, source branch, database metadata query, Preview deployment, provider status or HTTP 200 is **not** exact production certification. Production status requires direct evidence for the intended immutable revision plus the relevant authenticated end-to-end journey. External rate limits, missing secrets or inaccessible targets remain blockers; they are not reasons to weaken gates, fabricate state or incur unapproved spending.
