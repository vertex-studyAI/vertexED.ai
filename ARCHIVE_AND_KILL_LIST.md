## Compute allocation law

Until the kill list is revisited:

- at most **3** major scientific experiments concurrently;
- at most **2** major paper conversions concurrently;
- at most **2** major product validation programmes concurrently;
- a Tier B experiment runs only when all runnable Tier S/A dependencies are already active or blocked;
- no project gets additional compute merely because idle agents exist;
- a failed fair experiment usually buys **closure**, not an automatic successor.

## What is explicitly preserved

Archive never means deletion. Retain:

- failed and negative raw outputs
- frozen protocols and seed lists
- hashes and verifier output
- old branches/worktrees until safely classified
- bugged pre-repair results when scientifically relevant
- superseded manuscripts/status files with provenance
- exact source SHAs and dependency/environment records where available

The purpose of this list is to free attention, not erase history.
