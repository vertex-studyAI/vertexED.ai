## Evidence language rules

Use verbs that match the artifact:

- `built`, `implemented`, `tested`, `reproduced`, `falsified`, `released`, `deployed`, `used`, `published`, `accepted`, `validated` are **not interchangeable**.
- A negative result can be a strong research artifact when the protocol and evidence are rigorous.
- A repository or commit proves code/history, not user impact.
- A prepared manuscript or external-review packet is not a publication or external review result.
- Internal agents/reviewers are not independent external validators.
- User/adoption/revenue/organization-scale numbers require direct records before entering application wording.
