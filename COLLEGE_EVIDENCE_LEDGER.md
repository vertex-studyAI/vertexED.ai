**Application identity:** mathematical ML researcher-builder who creates serious systems, tests hypotheses honestly, ships products, and builds institutions around the work.

## Evidence law

For application use, separate four layers:

1. **Artifact fact** — directly evidenced by code, protocol, result, workflow, deployment metadata or retained package.
2. **Personal attribution** — who actually conceived, implemented, led, reviewed or maintained it. Git history alone is not enough for authorship/leadership claims.
3. **Scale/impact** — users, members, reach, revenue, delegates, citations, adoption, competition results or partner usage require outside evidence.
4. **Prestige/outcome** — publication, peer review, acceptance, grant, selective-program result or external validation require direct corresponding evidence.

Any missing layer stays `UNVERIFIED_OWNER_METADATA` or `EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE_PENDING`; it is never filled from inference.

## Application-language guardrails

Never write:

- “published” when a manuscript/release packet is only prepared;
- “peer-reviewed” for internal agent review;
- “externally validated” when only an outreach packet exists;
- “2,424 research projects completed” from a registry count;
- “1,000,000 agents” as physical concurrency;
- “production-ready” for a Preview, CI pass or source migration alone;
- user/member/reach/retention/revenue numbers without auditable external evidence;
- superiority claims for frozen negative/mixed lines.

Prefer language that states **what was built, what was tested, what failed, what evidence exists, and what remains blocked**. That is the strongest defensible version of this portfolio.
