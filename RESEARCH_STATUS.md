The current paper is a falsification/reproducibility paper. Current LAM main source-verifies that the frozen ARC path is narrower than the project label: same-input EMA target alignment, a non-contextual token path, and a one-step latent-action rollout. The result does not support family-level claims about JEPA, Transformers or planning.

### IRIS

The baseline-frontier protocol is now frozen around a strict `0.02` per-condition false-open eligibility rule, `TWMSE25`, recovery behavior, `POST_MSE50PLUS`, clean/heavy-tail error and an oracle-residual decision. It is **not authorized to execute** until exact canonical raw trajectories/implementations/configs are recovered. Confirmatory seeds remain forbidden.

### NeuroCAD
The frozen component diagnostic found `VALIDATION_DOMINANT`: matched fail-closed validation alone recovered 100% of the current direct-baseline gap on the reused 20-case diagnostic. Therefore the v1 difference is not evidence of a typed/parser causal mechanism on those cases. Software reliability remains valuable; research priority drops.

### JEPA × time-series
The portfolio does not create seven JEPA time-series projects. One programme question is retained: whether strictly causal future-latent prediction preferentially recovers predictive state under unpredictable noise/missingness versus masked latent prediction, reconstruction, autoregression, contrastive and strong statistical/state-space controls. H2 partial observability + H4 non-Gaussian noise are the first supporting conditions if the programme is ever activated. No experiment is currently authorized.
