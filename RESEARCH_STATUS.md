## Paper factory rule

A paper is promoted to `PAPER_DRAFT` only when its central empirical result has been measured and retained. A defensible candidate must include a precise question, related-work positioning, method, reproducible setup, credible baselines, metrics, experiments, meaningful ablations, results, limitations, figures/tables and reproduction instructions.

Negative and null results remain results. They are not rewritten as mechanism wins to make a paper sound stronger.
