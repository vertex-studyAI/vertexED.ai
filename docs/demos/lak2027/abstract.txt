Learning analytics depends on distinctions that can disappear before an educator sees a dashboard: a measured zero versus an absent score, an unfinished retry versus a completed attempt, and a withdrawn participant versus an active record. This interactive demonstration presents VertexED's observation review workflow for a study sequence of attempt, review, and retry. Six constructed session records make each distinction inspectable without using learner data.

Attendees replace a measured zero with a missing value, restore the observation, withdraw an example participant, and inspect the recomputed aggregate. The same repository analytics functions validate records and generate the downloadable output. Review actions connect rejected or incomplete observations to source repair and follow-up questions, while preserving uncertainty about learning. Aggregate exports omit participant identifiers and session rows; the demonstration also explains why aggregation alone cannot guarantee anonymity in small cohorts.

The contribution is a reproducible tool demonstration of transparent observation handling, accompanied by a runnable example and captioned walkthrough. Software checks assess data handling and interaction behaviour. They provide no evidence of educational efficacy, institutional adoption, or causal learning gains. The session invites learning analytics practitioners to examine which missing observations require action before interpreting a study summary or planning an educational intervention.
