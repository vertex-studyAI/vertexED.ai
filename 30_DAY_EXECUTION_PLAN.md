# 30-DAY EXECUTION PLAN

**Start:** 2026-08-14  
**End:** 2026-09-12  
**Objective:** fewer active projects, stronger evidence, paper/product closure, preserved negative results.

## Concurrency caps

At any moment:

- **≤3 major scientific experiments**
- **≤2 major paper conversions**
- **≤2 major product validation programs**

An externally blocked project does not consume an experimental slot unless it is actively executing a validated external test. Do not fill unused slots merely because capacity exists.

## JEPA × time-series program during these 30 days

Do not launch seven separate JEPA papers. The program remains a hypothesis-selection funnel.

Candidate hypotheses are:

- long-horizon latent state vs unpredictable observation noise;
- partial observability/missingness;
- regime shift transfer;
- heavy-tailed/non-Gaussian observation noise;
- irregular sampling;
- multivariate cross-variable dynamics;
- OOD representation transfer.

Only one child experiment may be promoted this month. It must have:

1. synthetic diagnostic that isolates the hypothesized mechanism;
2. one real benchmark;
3. strongest dangerous baseline, including simple statistical forecasting where appropriate;
4. reconstruction/autoregressive/contrastive/simple-latent controls;
5. frozen falsifier and kill criterion;
6. compute small enough to run multi-seed fairly;
7. value even if JEPA loses.

Given current evidence, **regime shift / partial observability / non-Gaussian noise** should be evaluated as candidate questions, but no one is declared the winner without a preregistered cheap falsification comparison.

## Daily operating loop

1. Recover actual state.
2. Verify artifacts/status deltas.
3. Rank by information gain × closure probability.
4. Execute smallest decisive task.
5. Independently verify.
6. Append artifacts/hashes/results.
7. Update claim ledger conservatively.
8. Promote, falsify, block or archive.
9. Prepare dependency-aware next queue.

No task is created merely to keep agents busy.
