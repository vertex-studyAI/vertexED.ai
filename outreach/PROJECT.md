# Outreach — Project

**Type:** Cross-portfolio business-development and relationship engine  
**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Primary objective:** Generate real conversations, calls, pilots, mentors, reviewers, sponsors, partnerships, customers, and research opportunities.

## Mission

Optimize for:

**RELEVANT TARGET → PERSONALIZED MESSAGE → REPLY → CALL → OUTCOME**

Raw send volume is not success.

## Operating rules

- No spam or duplicate asks.
- No deceptive personalization.
- Every contact needs a defensible reason for outreach.
- Track opt-outs and cooldowns; avoid repeated unwanted contact.
- Never claim relationships, traction, deployment, validation, or customer status that evidence does not support.
- Warm conversations and requested actions outrank cold expansion.
- Keep project/outcome lanes separate.

## Canonical lanes

### NeuroCAD
Design partners, mechanical/CAD engineers, product-development firms, tooling/fixture teams, manufacturing engineering teams, robotics/hardware teams. Current ask is a bounded engineering stress test, not an enterprise-sale claim.

### VertexED
Students, teachers, schools, tutoring organizations, then investors/programs. User and school evidence outranks investor volume.

### FinanceMeta
Universities, schools, fintech/data partners, sponsors, competition partners, judges.

### The Bu1LD
Professors/labs, compute/GPU/cloud partners, universities/schools for programs, AI/dev-tool sponsors, technical mentors.

### Research
LAM-JEPA, Project2424, IRIS, NGMT, Eigen-JEPA and related work: reviewers, replicators, domain experts, PhD students, labs, professors. These are validation/collaboration lanes, not customer acquisition.

### ObscuredRecords
Sponsors, distribution, media/syndication partners only after current analytics are re-verified. Historical reach stays historical until then.

### Cove / Shopify
Buyers, communities, distribution and affiliates. Track PRODUCT → TRAFFIC → CART → CHECKOUT → ORDER; do not mix this with professor/lab outreach.

### Percy / Olympus / Ultron
Build/validate first. External contact is validation/reviewer-oriented until product/scientific gates are satisfied.

## Success metrics

Primary: qualified replies, calls booked, pilots, collaborations, customers/orders, mentors, sponsors, reviewers.  
Secondary: response rate, follow-up completion, time to outcome.

## Required contact fields

TARGET, WHY_THEM, PROJECT, ASK, LAST_CONTACT, REPLY_STATUS, NEXT_ACTION, FOLLOW_UP_DATE, OUTCOME, EVIDENCE, OPT_OUT_OR_COOLDOWN.

## Truth labels

VERIFIED / LIKELY / UNKNOWN / BLOCKED / STALE / FAILED.
