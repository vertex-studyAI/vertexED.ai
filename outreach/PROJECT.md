# Outreach — Project

**Type:** Cross-portfolio business-development and relationship engine  
**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Primary objective:** Generate real conversations, calls, pilots, mentors, reviewers, sponsors, partnerships, customers, and research opportunities.

## Mission

**RELEVANT TARGET → PERSONALIZED MESSAGE → REPLY → CALL → OUTCOME**

Raw send volume is not success.

## Operating rules

- No spam or duplicate asks.
- No deceptive personalization.
- Every contact needs a defensible reason for outreach.
- Track opt-outs and cooldowns; avoid repeated unwanted contact.
- Never claim relationships, traction, deployment, validation, or customer status that evidence does not support.
- Warm conversations and requested actions outrank cold expansion.
- Keep project/outcome lanes separate.

## Canonical lanes

- **NeuroCAD:** bounded engineering stress tests and design partners; not enterprise-sales claims.
- **VertexED:** students, teachers, schools and tutoring organizations before investor volume.
- **FinanceMeta:** universities, schools, fintech/data partners, sponsors, competition partners and judges.
- **The Bu1LD:** professors/labs, compute/cloud partners, schools/programs, sponsors and technical mentors.
- **Research:** reviewers, replicators, domain experts and collaborators; not customers.
- **ObscuredRecords:** sponsors/distribution only after current analytics are re-verified.
- **Cove / Shopify:** buyers, communities, distribution and affiliates through a commerce funnel.
- **Percy / Olympus / Ultron:** validation/reviewer-oriented until product/scientific gates are satisfied.

## Success metrics

Primary: qualified replies, calls booked, completed pilots, collaborations, customers/orders, mentors, sponsors and reviewers.  
Secondary: response rate, follow-up completion and time to outcome.

## Required contact fields

TARGET, WHY_THEM, PROJECT, ASK, LAST_CONTACT, REPLY_STATUS, NEXT_ACTION, FOLLOW_UP_DATE, OUTCOME, EVIDENCE, OPT_OUT_OR_COOLDOWN.

## Truth labels

VERIFIED / LIKELY / UNKNOWN / BLOCKED / STALE / FAILED.
