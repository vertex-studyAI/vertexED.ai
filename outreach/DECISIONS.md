# Outreach — Decisions

## 2026-08-23

1. **Outcomes over volume.** Qualified replies, calls, completed pilots, customers/orders, collaborators, mentors, sponsors and reviewers are primary metrics.
2. **Project routing is mandatory.** Do not mix portfolio contacts into one campaign.
3. **Warm before cold.** Existing replies, scheduled calls, requested materials, live pilots and due follow-ups outrank new list expansion.
4. **NeuroCAD is bounded-design-partner work, not enterprise readiness.** Immutable public artifact publication is now proven, but public-browser and external-validation gates remain separate.
5. **VertexED users/schools outrank investor volume** while production/authenticated evidence remains incomplete.
6. **FinanceMeta is institutional partnership work** and remains access-gated for code/production changes in this execution surface.
7. **Research has reviewers/replicators, not customers.** Preserve falsification and negative results.
8. **Obscured sponsor outreach is gated by current analytics verification.** Historical reach stays historical until re-verified.
9. **Cove is buyer acquisition only after fulfillment is real.** Ten current drafts are not sell-ready while inventory is zero and supplier linkage is absent.
10. **Percy/Olympus/Ultron are validation-first.** Do not manufacture commercial green.
11. **Evidence defines green.** Issue creation, draft preparation, sends and plans alone are not final outcomes.
