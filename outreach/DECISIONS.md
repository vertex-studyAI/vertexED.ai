# Outreach — Decisions

## 2026-08-23

1. **Outcomes over volume.** Qualified replies, calls, completed pilots, customers/orders, collaborators, mentors, sponsors and reviewers are the primary metrics.
2. **Project routing is mandatory.** Do not mix all portfolio contacts into one campaign.
3. **NeuroCAD is design-partner ready, not enterprise-ready.** Use bounded one-engineer/one-part stress tests until deployment and external validation gates are satisfied.
4. **NeuroCAD deserves its own canonical repository.** Current embedded location in VertexED is temporary; preserve provenance when splitting it out.
5. **VertexED users/schools outrank investor volume.** Product and usage evidence should lead fundraising narrative.
6. **FinanceMeta is an institutional-partnership motion.** Universities, schools, data/API partners, sponsors, judges and competition partners are distinct lanes.
7. **Research has reviewers, not clients.** LAM-JEPA, Project2424, IRIS, NGMT, Eigen-JEPA and related work use bounded technical asks and claim-challenge/replication requests.
8. **Obscured sponsor outreach is gated by current analytics verification.** Historical reach claims remain explicitly historical.
9. **Cove is buyer acquisition.** Use an ecommerce funnel and do not cross-contaminate academic/research contacts.
10. **Percy/Olympus/Ultron are validation-first.** Do not force commercial green by selling prematurely.
11. **Warm-before-cold.** Existing replies, scheduled calls, requested materials, live pilots and due follow-ups outrank new list expansion.
12. **Evidence defines green.** Issue creation, draft preparation, and sends alone are not final outcomes.
