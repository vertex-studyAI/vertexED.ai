# The Bu1LD Build + Research Sprint — Public Pre-Launch

The Bu1LD Build + Research Sprint is a planned 10–14 day online sprint for teams that want to finish a real technical or research artifact rather than accumulate vague project activity.

## Tracks

- research replication or extension
- ML / scientific-computing prototype
- developer tool or product
- social-impact technology
- early startup MVP

## Entry standard

Before work begins, every team must define:

1. a concrete problem;
2. a falsifiable or testable goal;
3. a named output artifact;
4. an evidence standard for deciding whether the work succeeded, failed or remained inconclusive.

## Research-stack workflow

Where relevant, teams may use the emerging Bu1LD research stack:

**ATHENA** research-plan contract → **LEGO-ML** composable implementation → **FORGE** reproducibility record → **CRUCIBLE** benchmark contract → **ARGUS** audit → **LATTICE** provenance → **ATLAS** evidence publication.

These tools are previews; teams are not required to use them when a simpler reproducible workflow is better.

## Operating rhythm

- kickoff and scope freeze
- team/project matching
- technical and research clinics
- mentor office hours
- midpoint evidence review
- final submission and demo
- judging
- written postmortem

## Judging dimensions

- problem quality
- technical/research rigor
- usefulness
- execution
- evidence quality
- clarity of demo
- continuation potential

## Planned success targets

- 25+ active teams
- 15+ completed submissions
- 8+ strong demos or research artifacts
- 3–5 projects promoted into longer Bu1LD programs

## Integrity rule

A polished demo is not enough. Final submissions should state what was attempted, what actually worked, what failed, what evidence exists and what remains unverified. Negative and inconclusive results are acceptable when the process is rigorous.
