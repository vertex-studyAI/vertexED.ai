## Evidence discipline

Competition materials must distinguish:

- repository and production evidence;
- verified tests and deployment checks;
- self-reported users, partnerships, or reach;
- planned capabilities;
- credential-dependent journeys that remain unverified.

No application may claim a benchmark, customer count, deployment, partnership, or scientific result without a source that can be included in the evidence inventory.
