## Green criteria

An experiment is GREEN only when all applicable fields are explicit:

- canonical identity/source lineage;
- status/verdict;
- evidence/raw-result pointer;
- interpretation/claim boundary;
- reproducibility state;
- adverse/negative controls;
- next action or terminal disposition.

For an active successor, GREEN means **READY_TO_RUN under a completely frozen protocol**, not that a positive result exists.
