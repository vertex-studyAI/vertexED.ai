# Skeptical reviewer demands — top six Project 2424 lanes

These are the strongest predictable scientific objections after current-literature pressure testing.

## T2424-0027
- Language centering/removal already exists. What is the new empirical question?
- Does the effect hold on a real multilingual encoder and real multilingual task data?
- Does intent/semantic information remain intact?
- Do global centering, random grouping, random subspace, and established language-removal methods explain the result?

## T2424-0025
- Why is robust aggregation novel here rather than classical robust statistics?
- Why does the robust readout already help at zero contamination?
- Which mechanism is actually isolated?
- Does the effect persist in an actual memory/sequence model rather than a synthetic precursor?

## T2424-1863
- What published/general scientific claim does this negative screen inform?
- Why is the >75% threshold scientifically meaningful?
- Does the result generalize beyond the bounded synthetic local setup?
- Without broader baselines, why is this a conference paper rather than a reproducibility note?

## T2424-0050
- Where are FNO/DeepONet/reduced-order dangerous baselines?
- Does the interpretable harmonic compression survive misalignment and OOD?
- Does it work beyond 1D?
- Why should this be considered operator learning rather than a physics-derived coarse solver baseline?

## T2424-0037 NeuroCAD
- The historical benchmark is narrow; how does the result survive 2026 Text-to-CAD benchmarks?
- Does matched validation erase the apparent typed-representation advantage?
- Are evaluation cases truly held out?
- Are model/provider and dataset identities immutable before evaluation?

## NGMT v0.1
- External memory in Transformers is established; what specific robust-memory mechanism is new?
- Is the negative result stable beyond three paired seeds and a tiny synthetic setup?
- Does contamination severity or sequence length change the conclusion?
- Is there a real long-context task where the mechanism can be falsified under matched capacity?

Any paper that cannot answer its section above should not be promoted to flagship conference readiness.
