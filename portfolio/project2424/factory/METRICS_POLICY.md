# Metrics and Statistics Policy

Every SPECIFIED empirical project must identify one primary metric before confirmatory analysis. Secondary metrics may clarify behavior but cannot silently replace a failed primary endpoint.

Where stochasticity matters, report seed-level results and an uncertainty summary appropriate to the design. Where paired comparisons are possible, prefer paired analysis. Statistical tests must match assumptions and sample structure.

A metric definition, aggregation rule, or threshold changed after observing the frozen result creates a new protocol version and may not overwrite the original verdict.
