# Independent Paper Reviewer Checklist

Reviewer answers YES/NO with evidence:

- Is the research question precise and falsifiable?
- Is the closest prior work represented fairly and currently?
- Are baselines credible and matched?
- Was the confirmatory protocol frozen before interpretation?
- Are all headline values traceable to raw artifacts?
- Are uncertainty/statistics appropriate?
- Do ablations support any mechanism claim?
- Are negative/mixed findings visible in the main paper?
- Are scope and external-validity boundaries explicit?
- Can the headline result be independently reproduced?
- Does the abstract match the evidence?
- Are authorship, licensing, and data statements accurate?

Any material NO blocks PREPRINT_READY.
