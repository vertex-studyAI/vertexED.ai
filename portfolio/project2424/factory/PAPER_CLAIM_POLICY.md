# Paper Claim Policy

Use the weakest wording that is fully supported by the frozen evidence.

- `synthetic` stays synthetic.
- `historical` stays historical.
- `controlled diagnostic` is not held-out generalization.
- `mechanism` requires isolation evidence.
- `better than baseline` requires a credible matched baseline and correct uncertainty/statistics.
- `generalizes` requires actual out-of-distribution or external evidence.
- `state of the art` requires a current, apples-to-apples benchmark audit.
- `causes` requires evidence stronger than correlation.

When the result is negative/mixed/inconclusive, say so in the abstract and Results rather than burying it in limitations.
