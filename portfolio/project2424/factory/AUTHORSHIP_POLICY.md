# Authorship / Contributor Policy

Authorship is not inferred from repository access or generated boilerplate. Before preprint release, record actual contributors and roles (conceptualization, methodology, software, validation, analysis, data, writing, supervision, etc.).

Do not list a person as an author without their consent and material contribution. Automated agents/tools are acknowledged according to applicable venue policy rather than fabricated as human authors. Resolve institutional names, conflicts, acknowledgements and funding statements before release.
