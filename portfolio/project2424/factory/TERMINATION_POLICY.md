# Project Termination Policy

A child can terminate without a preprint when the premise is invalid, irretrievably duplicated, unethical/unavailable to test, or too underspecified to recover. Termination must preserve:

- the reason;
- the evidence/review that led to termination;
- any useful negative finding;
- lineage/duplicate links;
- what would be required to reopen it.

Termination is preferable to fabricating a contribution or paper.
