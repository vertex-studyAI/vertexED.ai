## Rules

- No evidence artifact → claim is not allowed.
- A synthetic result must remain labeled synthetic.
- A mechanism claim requires an isolating ablation or equivalent causal evidence.
- A superiority claim requires credible matched baselines.
- A real-world/generalization claim requires evidence outside the development diagnostic when applicable.
- Negative/mixed/inconclusive findings must not be omitted when they materially qualify the headline claim.
