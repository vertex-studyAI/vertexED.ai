# Dashboard Count Rules

Counts must be mutually interpretable and source-backed.

- `named` means canonical identity resolved.
- `implemented` means project-specific code/analytic workflow exists and runs.
- `experimented` means the frozen/main protocol was executed.
- `result_frozen` means outcome and artifacts are immutable/versioned.
- `reproduced` means independent reproduction passed.
- `paper_drafted` means a full manuscript exists, not just a template.
- `preprint_ready` means all release gates pass.
- `preprinted` means a public preprint identifier/URL exists.

Never infer one state from a weaker state.
