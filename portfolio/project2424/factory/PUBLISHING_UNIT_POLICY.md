# Child Project vs Publication Unit

Project 2424 may contain 2,424 real child experiments without requiring 2,424 standalone papers.

A publication unit is determined by scientific independence and contribution, not child-count targets. One paper may synthesize multiple related children when they answer one coherent question; a child may become its own paper when its question/mechanism/evidence is independently substantial.

Track both counts separately:

- child research states;
- standalone/family manuscript states.

Never inflate paper counts by splitting one experiment family into minimally different manuscripts.
