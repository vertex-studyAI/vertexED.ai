# Literature / Novelty Review Policy

Before a child moves to SPECIFIED or a manuscript is finalized:

1. search current literature using the exact mechanism/task vocabulary;
2. record the closest 3–10 works and any benchmark released after the original project idea;
3. distinguish novelty of method, evaluation, dataset, mechanism, and negative finding;
4. update the research question if prior work already answers the original claim;
5. do not call a method novel or state-of-the-art without a current evidence-backed comparison.

A newer benchmark can materially change the paper design and should be incorporated when relevant.
