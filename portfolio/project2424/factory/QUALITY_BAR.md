# Quality Bar

The target is not "looks like a paper." The target is a technically competent reader can answer:

- What exactly was hypothesized?
- What would have falsified it?
- What data/environment and baselines were used?
- What happened under the frozen protocol?
- How uncertain/robust is the result?
- What failed?
- Can I reproduce it?
- Which claims are and are not supported?
- How does this differ from current prior work?

If the package cannot answer these, it is not preprint quality.
