# Dangerous Baseline Policy

A Project 2424 paper must compare against the simplest credible method likely to invalidate the proposed contribution.

Baseline selection cannot be designed merely to make the new method look good. Where applicable include:

- simple linear/ridge/logistic models;
- direct generation plus matched validation/repair;
- matched parameter/compute budgets;
- classical numerical/analytic methods;
- nearest current published benchmark baselines;
- no-ML or persistence/heuristic controls.

If a simple baseline wins, that is the result.
