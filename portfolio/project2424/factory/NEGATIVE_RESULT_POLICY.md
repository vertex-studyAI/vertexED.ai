# Negative-Result Publication Policy

A failed hypothesis is a valid Project 2424 result when the experiment is well-specified, reproducible, and informative.

Do not:
- lower a frozen threshold after seeing the result;
- cherry-pick seeds to reverse the verdict;
- remove a stronger baseline;
- hide null/negative ablations;
- use locked test data to tune a failed development hypothesis.

Do:
- report the pre-specified gate;
- quantify uncertainty and failure modes;
- explain what hypothesis was ruled out and what remains open;
- release reproduction evidence when licensing/data constraints permit.
