# Claim — T2424-0027

## Falsifiable mechanics claim

On the frozen balanced synthetic latent generator, subtracting each language's **training-set centroid** will reduce normalized excess language-label predictability by at least **90%** while preserving concept-label nearest-centroid accuracy within **2 percentage points** of the raw latent baseline.

The raw construction must first make both concept signal and language leakage detectable at **>=95%** accuracy. As a negative control, subtracting one global training centroid must retain **>=95%** language-label accuracy; otherwise the experiment would not show that the language-specific transform, rather than arbitrary centering, caused the leakage reduction.

## Claim boundary

A pass supports only the controlled evaluator/transform mechanics on the deterministic synthetic construction. It is **not evidence** for the Sapir–Whorf hypothesis, linguistic relativity, semantic universals, real multilingual model behavior, learned language-agnostic representations, translation quality, cultural cognition, or publication novelty.
