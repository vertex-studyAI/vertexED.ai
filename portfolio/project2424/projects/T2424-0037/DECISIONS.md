# NeuroCAD — Decision Ledger

## D001 — Separate research and product lines

**Decision:** Product hardening may continue even though the historical typed-IR mechanism claim was falsified.

**Reason:** The matched-validation diagnostic identifies validation as the dominant explanation for the historical bounded-case gap. That does not invalidate the usefulness of a structured, validated, editable product architecture.

## D002 — Preserve `VALIDATION_DOMINANT`

**Decision:** The old typed-IR/parser superiority claim remains falsified unless a genuinely new pre-frozen experiment changes the evidence.

**Consequence:** Product PRs cannot change research verdicts.

## D003 — Narrow engineering-object demo first

**Decision:** Use the editable flanged-tube workflow as the primary credibility demo.

**Reason:** It demonstrates text -> explicit parameters -> validation -> geometry -> stateful edit/regeneration -> export with a small inspectable object.

## D004 — Conceptual assembly is visual proof, not the core scientific claim

**Decision:** Keep the multi-part conceptual assembly as a visualization/interaction showcase, with explicit scope boundaries.

## D005 — Fail closed

**Decision:** Unsupported or contradictory instructions must return understandable diagnostics rather than silently generating guessed geometry.

## D006 — Do not weaken G12

**Decision:** Public Alpha is not green until the live public route serves the intended artifact identity and the production browser smoke passes.

**Reason:** Source-level CI success is not proof that production serves that source.

## D007 — Outreach is not validation

**Decision:** A pilot counts only after an independent external engineer/team actually exercises a bounded workflow and returns observable evidence.

## D008 — Pilot evidence chooses scope expansion

**Decision:** Do not add broad CAD features speculatively. After initial pilots, prioritize the smallest missing capability that repeatedly blocks real workflows.

## D009 — New science requires a new evidence lineage

**Decision:** Any renewed typed-IR scientific claim must use a new predeclared benchmark/baseline/model freeze and cannot recycle the historical 20-case study as confirmatory evidence.

## D010 — Keep claims narrower than implementation

**Decision:** Even when a backend can emit geometry, do not promote manufacturing/interoperability claims without dedicated evidence for those properties.
