## Workflow questions

1. Was the generated geometry inspectable enough to understand what NeuroCAD believed you asked for?
2. Were dimensions and parameters preserved correctly across edits?
3. When NeuroCAD rejected a request, was the rejection understandable?
4. What was the first point where you would have gone back to your normal CAD tool?
5. What single capability would make you test NeuroCAD again?
6. Did NeuroCAD ever accept an instruction it should have rejected?

## Evidence rule

Do not convert this session into a product claim unless the exact workflow, revision, result, and any limitations are preserved. Negative and mixed results remain part of the pilot record.
