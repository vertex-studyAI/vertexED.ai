RESEARCH RECORD
NEGATIVE RESULTS STAY VISIBLE
NeuroCAD began as a bounded NLP→CAD experiment. Its frozen held-out v1 benchmark recorded 95% overall success versus 60% for the original direct flat extractor. A later pre-frozen component ablation showed that a direct extractor with matched fail-closed validation recovered the full measured gap. The supported conclusion is therefore validation-dominant engineering reliability on that diagnostic—not a demonstrated typed-IR causal advantage.
