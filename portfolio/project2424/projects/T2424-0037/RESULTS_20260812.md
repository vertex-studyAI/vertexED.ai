## Research question

Within this controlled-language CAD setting, does an explicit validated intermediate representation improve constraint adherence and fail-closed behavior relative to simpler direct text-to-CAD emission baselines?

## Frozen benchmark v0.1

- 26 deterministic prompts total;
- 14 expected-valid prompts;
- 12 expected-rejection prompts;
- positive cases cover rectangular plates/blocks, default and explicit thickness, decimal dimensions, 1/2/4-hole layouts, diameter/radius normalization and wording variants;
- negative cases cover unsupported cylinders/gears, missing dimensions, unsupported hole counts, unsafe dimensions, impossible hole geometry, missing hole size, zero thickness and code-like prompt fragments.

## Three architectural baselines

1. `template_only` — narrow exact-template emission with no general constraint layer.
2. `direct_regex` — extracts dimensions and directly emits OpenSCAD without the validated structured hole/constraint representation.
3. `structured_ir` — controlled-language parse → normalized parametric IR → geometry validation → OpenSCAD.

These are deterministic engineering baselines, not language-model baselines.

## Failure taxonomy

- `unsupported_family` — requested geometry family is outside the bounded compiler, e.g. cylinder or gear;
- `underspecified` — required dimensions or hole size are absent;
- `invalid_constraint` — unsupported hole count, non-positive dimensions or impossible hole geometry;
- `safety` — dimensions exceed the safety envelope or prompt contains code-like syntax;
- `parser_coverage` — a baseline rejects a valid wording form;
- `geometry_mismatch` — a baseline accepts but omits or misstates required geometry.

## Interpretation

The observed result supports the narrow claim that, **on this authored controlled benchmark**, the explicit validated IR pipeline is more reliable than the two simpler deterministic emission baselines. It does not establish that an IR improves arbitrary natural-language CAD generation, because the benchmark and compiler were developed together and the direct baseline is deliberately simple.

## Next research gate
