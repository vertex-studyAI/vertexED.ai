# NeuroCAD Alpha 0.1 — Safety and Scope

NeuroCAD Alpha is a conceptual/educational parametric geometry system. The jet-engine demo is deliberately bounded to visual assembly geometry.

## Supported public-alpha claims
- Constrained engineering-language commands can be parsed into explicit intents.
- Supported intents can generate validated parametric geometry.
- The jet-engine concept is a deterministic multi-component assembly built from bounded primitives.
- Users can inspect the assembly, alter bounded visual parameters, change casing visibility/exploded view, and export a validated CADSpec JSON artifact.
- The historical rectangular-plate lineage has separate retained OpenSCAD/STL execution evidence.

## Explicit non-claims
The alpha does not establish or provide:
- airworthiness or certification;
- manufacturing readiness/correctness;
- structural, thermal, fatigue, vibration, or stress validation;
- CFD or FEA;
- combustion modeling, injector/fuel-system design, ignition, flame stabilization, or operating fuel flow;
- thrust, compression ratio, RPM, turbine inlet temperature, efficiency, fuel consumption, or propulsion performance;
- arbitrary text-to-CAD generalization;
- superiority over contemporary learned CAD/program-generation systems;
- a typed-IR causal advantage from the historical benchmark.

## Historical research integrity
The frozen v1 held-out benchmark and the later `VALIDATION_DOMINANT` component ablation remain immutable evidence families. Product improvements must not rewrite either result.
