# FM:X — FinanceMeta Black Box Research Challenge

**Working public line:** We built an economy. Figure out how it works.

FM:X is a research competition in which teams investigate a synthetic financial system without being told the hidden mechanism that generated its behavior. Teams receive data, partial documentation and a mystery. They must form falsifiable hypotheses, test competing explanations, use limited experiment credits strategically, build a quantitative model and predict outcomes in an unseen version of the economy.

## What makes it different

The competition is designed to reward research ability rather than polished essay writing. A strong explanation must survive new data, counterfactual interventions and a hidden holdout test.

## First-edition structure

1. **Registration + practice** — teams receive rules, integrity requirements and a practice dataset.
2. **Discovery** — Dataset A; identify an anomaly, propose a hypothesis and state what would falsify it.
3. **Hypothesis challenge** — Dataset B changes the initial conditions and tests whether the theory survives.
4. **Experiment market** — each team receives five Experiment Credits for approved interventions.
5. **Research report + model** — teams submit their mechanism, evidence, alternatives, experiments, model and uncertainty.
6. **Hidden holdout** — teams submit predictions before outcomes are revealed.
7. **Final defense + truth reveal** — finalists defend their research, then FinanceMeta reveals the simulator mechanism.

## Divisions

- **Explorer Division:** high-school friendly, cleaned data, starter notebook and narrower guidance.
- **Research Division:** advanced high-school and undergraduate teams, fuller data and stronger quantitative expectations.
- **Open Division:** optional only if operations can support advanced/custom work.

## Scoring — 100 points

- Scientific discovery — 25
- Research reasoning — 20
- Evidence and experiments — 20
- Hidden-test performance — 20
- Economic interpretation — 10
- Communication and defense — 5

## Submission package

- 3,000–5,000 word research report, excluding references/technical appendix
- maximum 300-word executive research abstract
- reproducibility package with code/notebook/dependencies/instructions
- standardized hidden-test prediction file
- concise research log showing hypothesis changes and Experiment Credits used
- finalist presentation for live defense

## AI policy

FM:X is not designed as an “AI-free” competition. Material AI use must be disclosed, and winning work must still survive code/reproducibility requirements, hidden data, experiment choices and live adversarial questioning.

## Integrity requirements

The simulator, released datasets and holdouts must be generated from locked code with recorded seeds. Teams must submit evidence for core claims. FinanceMeta should not publish the hidden mechanism until the finalist truth reveal.
