## Current strongest manuscript target

### Latent Law Compilation under Uncertain Dimensional Metadata

**Question:** how should symbolic model discovery use scientific dimensional knowledge when that knowledge may itself be uncertain?

Required structure:

1. Introduction — bounded problem and failure mode.
2. Related work — dimensional symbolic regression, sparse law discovery, model selection/MDL, uncertainty-aware scientific inference.
3. Method — deterministic dimensional compiler; hard-metadata failure mode; probabilistic extension; matched evidence contract.
4. Experimental setup — task/data contract, expression grammar, corruption process, seeds, budgets, hardware, metrics and baseline tuning.
5. Results — generated only from canonical raw artifacts.
6. Ablations — perfect metadata, corrupted metadata, uniform/calibrated uncertainty, no dimensional constraint, matched budgets.
7. Limitations — controlled expression family, real-world gap, grammar dependence, uncertain units, no SOTA claim, no independent replication.
8. Discussion — scientific structure helps only when its reliability is represented honestly.
9. Conclusion — one mechanism contribution, one failure mode, one uncertainty-aware extension.
10. Reproducibility appendix — configs, environment, seeds, raw schema, commands, checksums and negative results.
