## Frozen question

Can a history-conditioned bounded-influence recurrent write improve robustness to isolated heavy-tailed observation corruption relative to a matched Huber write while preserving Huber-level clean accuracy and adaptation to persistent location shifts?

The package explicitly rejects the broader framing of IRIS as a novel generic Student-t/heavy-tail memory architecture.

## Conclusion

**GREEN for evidence integrity / negative-result preservation; NOT GREEN for a successful IRIS mechanism.**

IRIS v0.2 is currently strongest as a defensible negative-result research package: its scalar mechanism is reproducible, the learned candidate fails clean/regime promotion, and the reserved confirmatory set remains untouched. The next mechanism version, if any, must be motivated from the pre-existing failure taxonomy and versioned separately before additional holdout evaluation.
