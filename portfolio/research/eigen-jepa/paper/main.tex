\begin{abstract}
We evaluate whether the project-named Eigen-JEPA spectral representation improves next-block covariance forecasting relative to direct statistical baselines on a frozen real-market task. The retained dataset contains 14,895 daily Fama--French five-factor observations from 1963-07-01 through 2022-08-31, aggregated into non-overlapping 20-day covariance blocks with four previous blocks as context and the next block as target. Chronological splitting yields 111 held-out test blocks. On the frozen primary matrix-MSE metric, Eigen-JEPA obtains $5.8318226\times10^{-9}$, compared with $5.7734384\times10^{-9}$ for raw covariance ridge and $5.7896089\times10^{-9}$ for log-covariance ridge. The retained paired Eigen-JEPA-minus-raw-ridge matrix-MSE difference is approximately $+5.8384\times10^{-11}$, with a retained 95\% interval of $[-2.3565\times10^{-10},\,4.1744\times10^{-10}]$, which crosses zero. Eigen-JEPA is better than persistence ($7.7708315\times10^{-9}$) on this task but does not establish superiority over the stronger ridge controls. We retain this adverse comparison rather than switch the primary metric or select a favorable period after inspection. The result motivates a separately preregistered successor; it does not justify retuning the present study in place.
\end{abstract}

Non-overlapping 20-day covariance blocks are formed; four previous blocks provide context and the next block is the forecast target. Splits are chronological. Compared methods are persistence, raw covariance ridge, log-covariance ridge, a Cholesky representation, and the Eigen-JEPA spectral/PCA log-space representation. Covariance-matrix MSE is the primary metric. Log-distance is secondary and descriptive where available. The study is judged on the frozen primary metric rather than a post-outcome alternative.

Across the 111 held-out blocks, the retained paired Eigen-JEPA-minus-raw-ridge matrix-MSE difference is approximately $+5.8384\times10^{-11}$. The frozen claim verdict is therefore mixed/negative. Eigen-JEPA does not establish superiority over raw ridge or log ridge on the primary metric. Its narrower improvement over persistence is retained as a task-specific observation and is not promoted into a broader market claim.

\section{Interpretation}
The result is decision-useful because representation complexity alone does not establish forecasting value. Covariance prediction admits strong direct structural and persistence baselines, and a learned spectral representation must beat the strongest relevant controls under a precommitted metric hierarchy before a superiority claim is justified. The present result supports neither a general rejection of spectral representations nor a claim that direct ridge methods dominate other markets or frequencies.

The secondary log-distance ordering cannot rescue the primary result. Log ridge has the lowest retained log-distance ($0.1506057$), followed by Eigen-JEPA ($0.1595020$) and raw ridge ($0.1945980$). Because covariance-matrix MSE is the frozen primary metric, this secondary ordering remains descriptive only.

\section{Related-Work Boundary}
The retained baseline set is narrower than the broader covariance-estimation and dynamic-correlation literature. Ledoit and Wolf's shrinkage estimator provides a classical route to better-conditioned high-dimensional covariance estimation~\cite{ledoitwolf2004}. Engle's dynamic conditional correlation model provides a parsimonious time-varying correlation framework~\cite{engle2002}. Laloux et al. use random-matrix theory to characterize noise in empirical financial correlation spectra~\cite{laloux1999}.

None of those methods was executed as a matched comparator in the retained frozen study. They are context, not retroactive baselines. This artifact therefore does not claim that Eigen-JEPA beats shrinkage covariance estimation, DCC-type models, random-matrix cleaning, or other unexecuted spectral/time-series methods. Adding such baselines after observing the retained outcome would define a new scientific protocol; a successor must freeze them prospectively.

\section{Limitations and Next Gate}
The retained study covers one bundled real-market dataset and historical period, one covariance-block construction, one chronological split family, and a limited baseline set. It does not span multiple markets, frequencies, transaction-cost settings, live trading, or distribution-shift regimes. Stronger shrinkage, spectral, dynamic-correlation, and time-series comparators remain unexecuted. No external reproduction has been completed.

Any successor must be a new preregistration. Before outcome access it should freeze a multi-dataset or multi-market hierarchy; exact primary and secondary metrics; stronger direct, shrinkage, dynamic-correlation, spectral/random-matrix, and time-series baselines; chronological train/validation/test identities and OOD periods; seed and aggregation policy; any practical-effect or statistical decision rules; exact environment/source/artifact identities; and a prospective falsifier. Metric switching, favorable-period selection, baseline removal, or in-place rescue tuning after observing successor outcomes is prohibited.

\section{Conclusion}
The current Eigen-JEPA study does not establish covariance-forecasting superiority. On the frozen held-out task, Eigen-JEPA records matrix MSE $5.8318226\times10^{-9}$, versus $5.7734384\times10^{-9}$ for raw ridge and $5.7896089\times10^{-9}$ for log ridge. The paired primary comparison against raw ridge does not favor Eigen-JEPA and its retained 95\% interval crosses zero. Eigen-JEPA is better than persistence on this task, but that narrower observation does not rescue the stronger superiority claim. The study should remain frozen as mixed/negative real-data evidence; any broader claim requires a separately preregistered successor.
