## External-validation dispatch rule

Before sending anything outside:

1. freeze the exact claim being validated;
2. freeze the artifact/version being sent;
3. request a bounded action, not general praise;
4. record negative or ambiguous feedback intact;
5. never label outreach, a sent email, or a pending review as validation;
6. do not choose only validators likely to agree.
