## Language law

Use `GREEN — reproducible negative result` when the experiment/reproduction is complete but the hypothesis or mechanism failed. A subsequent software repair does not rewrite a frozen scientific result. New claims require new versioned protocols.

`Independent runner`, `independent verifier`, or `independent audit` inside the same project/control environment is **internal verification**, not external reproduction. Use `external reproduction` only for a genuinely independent operator/environment/reimplementation with retained provenance.
