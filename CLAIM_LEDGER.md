## Claim-freeze contract

For Tier-S/A research projects, `CLAIM_LEDGER.json` now records:

- one precise scientific question;
- one falsifiable hypothesis;
- the mechanism actually proposed/tested;
- a conservative novelty boundary;
- the primary supported claim;
- explicit non-claims;
- a falsifier;
- dangerous baseline families;
- the next authorized gate.

A later result may update **support**, but a failed frozen hypothesis is never rewritten after the fact. A changed question, mechanism, threshold, seed policy, baseline family or confirmatory protocol requires a versioned successor record.

## Language rule

Use `GREEN — reproducible negative result` when execution/reproduction is complete but the scientific hypothesis failed. Do not shorten that to `GREEN model` or `GREEN superiority`. Keep implementation, source, reproducibility, production, external validation, submission, publication and acceptance/finalist states separate.
