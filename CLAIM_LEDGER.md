## Claim-freeze contract

For Tier-S/A research work, `CLAIM_LEDGER.json` records the precise question, hypothesis, mechanism, novelty boundary, primary claim, non-claims, falsifier, dangerous baseline families and next authorized gate.

A later result may update support, but a failed frozen hypothesis is never rewritten after the fact. Changed question, mechanism, threshold, data role, seed policy, baseline family or confirmatory protocol requires a versioned successor.

## Language law

Use `GREEN — reproducible negative result` when execution/reproduction is complete but the scientific hypothesis failed. Keep implementation, reproducibility, production, external validation, submission and publication states separate.
