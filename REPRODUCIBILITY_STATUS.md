## Evidence-chain law

For a quantitative claim to be reproducibility-ready, preserve a traceable chain:

`claim → table/figure → processed result → raw result → config/protocol → code/source identity → environment/data identity`

A draft, registry row, test badge, project name, or agent report is not a reproduced scientific claim. Adverse controls, invalid attempts and negative findings remain part of the evidence chain rather than being deleted when they reduce the headline result.
