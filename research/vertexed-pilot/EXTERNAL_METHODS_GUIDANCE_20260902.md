# VertexED pilot — external methods guidance record (2026-09-02)

**Status:** PRE-OUTCOME METHODS NOTE / DOES NOT ALTER THE FROZEN PILOT PRIMARY OUTCOME

## Purpose

Record external methodological guidance received before any authorized human-subject study is treated as research evidence, without silently rewriting `VERTEXED_CONTROLLED_PILOT_PROTOCOL_202609.md`.

## Guidance captured

An external education research scientist advised that, for a study intended to assess whether the application is an effective learning tool, longer-term learning gains measured with an accepted standardized measure are a reasonable dependent-variable direction. The same guidance emphasized consulting the school/teachers about human-subject protections before conducting a study with an external group.

## Effect on the current September product pilot

The existing controlled-pilot protocol remains unchanged by this note:

- its frozen primary outcome is the paired difference in immediate percentage-point learning gain;
- delayed retention remains secondary in that protocol;
- engagement, completion, return intent, and in-app usage are not substitutes for learning evidence;
- a positive bounded pilot does not authorize a broad efficacy claim;
- negative, mixed, or inconclusive outcomes must be retained unchanged.

Do **not** promote delayed/standardized learning gain to the current primary outcome after outcomes have been inspected. If the team wants that endpoint to be primary, create a separately versioned protocol or explicit pre-outcome amendment with its own timestamp/hash before participant outcomes are viewed.

## Human-subjects gate

Before any school/external cohort is run as formal research evidence:

1. route the proposed activity through the school/teacher administrative pathway;
2. determine the applicable consent/guardian, privacy, data-handling, and ethics/oversight requirements;
3. document that determination before recruitment or outcome collection intended for research/publication;
4. keep product testing, internal QA, and formal human-subject research clearly distinguished in claims and evidence packages.

## Next evidence-safe design step

For a future confirmatory protocol, predeclare an accepted standardized or externally anchored learning measure and a delayed assessment interval before outcomes, while retaining the current September pilot as a bounded product-learning pilot under its existing claim ladder.
