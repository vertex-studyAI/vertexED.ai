## Choose a relationship before calculating

Two quantities are directly proportional when their ratio stays constant: y=kx. Doubling x then doubles y, and the graph passes through the origin within the model's domain. A fixed fee plus a charge per item is not direct proportion because the fixed fee does not scale with the item count. Find k from a known pair, keep its units, and test another pair if data are available. A straight line alone is insufficient evidence for direct proportionality unless the intercept is zero.

## Inverse proportion and what stays fixed

Inverse proportion means a product remains constant: xy=k. For a fixed distance, speed multiplied by travel time equals that distance, so doubling speed halves time only if the distance and the model's assumptions remain unchanged. Do not apply the same argument automatically to workers and completion time: coordination, limited equipment and different work rates may break it. State what is being held fixed before using either proportional model. The word increases does not by itself identify a proportional relationship or its mathematical form.

## Length, area, volume and units

For geometrically similar objects, corresponding lengths have scale factor s, areas have scale factor s squared, and volumes have scale factor s cubed. These statements require similarity, not merely a larger drawing or container. An area ratio of nine corresponds to a length ratio of three for positive scale factors. Compound units also encode relationships: mass divided by volume gives density, while distance divided by time gives speed. Convert units before division, and distinguish a unit conversion from a physical change in the measured object.

## Worked example 1

An ideal constant-price fabric costs 18 units for 2.4 m. Find the price of 3.2 m.

1. Find the rate: 18/2.4=7.5 units per metre.

2. Multiply 7.5 by 3.2 and check the units cancel metres.

Answer: 24 units.

## Worked example 2

Similar boxes have corresponding lengths in ratio 2:5. The smaller volume is 64 cm³. Find the larger volume.

1. The linear enlargement factor is 5/2.

2. Volume scales by (5/2)³=125/8; multiply this by 64.

Answer: 1,000 cm³.

## Misconceptions

- All increasing relationships are direct proportion.
  Correction: A constant ratio, not just a common direction of change, is required.

- A length factor of four gives a volume factor of four.
  Correction: For similar solids the volume factor is 4³=64.

## Flashcards

### What stays constant in direct proportion?
The ratio y/x, where x is nonzero.

### What stays constant in inverse proportion?
The product xy.

### Does y=3x+2 represent direct proportion?
No. The nonzero fixed term prevents a constant ratio.

### How does area scale in similar figures?
By the square of the positive length scale factor.

### What is the unit of mass divided by volume?
A density unit, such as g/cm³.

## Practice 1 — standard · 2 marks

In a direct-proportion model y=15 when x=6. Find y when x=14 and state the constant.

Answer: k=2.5 and y=35.

1. Divide 15 by 6 to determine k.

2. Substitute x=14 into y=2.5x.

Marking points (one practice mark each):

- Find k=2.5.

- Find y=35.

## Practice 2 — standard · 2 marks

A machine completes a fixed idealised job in 12 minutes at rate 5 items per minute. At 8 items per minute, how long does the same job take?

Answer: 7.5 minutes.

1. The job contains 12×5=60 items.

2. Divide 60 by 8; rate and time are inversely proportional for this fixed job.

Marking points (one practice mark each):

- Find the fixed quantity 60.

- Calculate 7.5 minutes.

## Practice 3 — extension · 2 marks

Two similar triangles have areas 20 and 45 cm². A side of the smaller is 8 cm. Find its corresponding larger side.

Answer: 12 cm.

1. The area ratio is 45/20=2.25.

2. The length factor is √2.25=1.5; multiply 8 by 1.5.

Marking points (one practice mark each):

- Take the square root of the area ratio.

- Find 12 cm.

## Practice 4 — standard · 2 marks

A delivery costs C=6+2n units for n parcels. A learner says twice as many parcels always costs twice as much. Test n=3 and explain the error.

Answer: Three parcels cost 12; six cost 18, not 24. The fixed fee is paid once.

1. Evaluate the expression at 3 and 6.

2. Identify the nonzero intercept as the reason the relationship is not direct proportion.

Marking points (one practice mark each):

- Give both costs.

- Explain the fixed-fee effect.
