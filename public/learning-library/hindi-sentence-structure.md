## Sentence structure and present descriptions

In a neutral Hindi statement, the main verb or copula commonly comes near the end. The sentence मैं छात्र हूँ means I am a male student; मैं छात्रा हूँ uses the feminine noun for a female student. The copula agrees with the subject: मैं हूँ, तुम हो, आप हैं, and वह है are useful starting patterns. ये हैं and वे हैं are used for plural reference, and respectful reference can also use plural agreement. Hindi permits changes in word order for emphasis and context, so subject-first and verb-last is a helpful beginner pattern rather than an absolute rule for every utterance. Learn complete meaningful examples instead of replacing English words one by one.

## Postpositions follow the noun phrase

English often places a preposition before a noun, as in in the room. Hindi commonly uses a postposition after the noun phrase: कमरे में means in the room, and मेज़ पर means on the table. Some nouns change form before a postposition: कमरा becomes कमरे in कमरे में. This is an example of an oblique form, not a plural merely because the final vowel changed. के साथ expresses with, as in मित्र के साथ, with a friend. The whole phrase functions together in the sentence. When reading, locate the postposition and the phrase it belongs to before deciding the relationship between the people, objects or places.

## Agreement, respect and checking meaning

A respectful form of address is a grammatical choice, not simply an extra word meaning please. आप normally takes हैं in a present description, as in आप शिक्षक हैं. Some adjectives change with gender and number while others do not; अच्छा and अच्छी illustrate a changing pair. In यह किताब अच्छी है, the feminine noun किताब is described by अच्छी. Do not assume every adjective must change, or that a noun's English translation reveals its grammatical gender. For a beginner writing task, choose vocabulary whose gender and ordinary use you have checked. Review the subject, the ending of the description and the placement of each postposition separately. Read the completed sentence for meaning as well as agreement, because a grammatically plausible sentence can still say something different from what the task requested.

## Meaning-preserving editing and connected descriptions

When editing a beginner Hindi description, first identify the intended referents and relationships. The order of words may differ from an English translation, so look for the whole noun phrase and its following postposition. In किताब मेज़ पर है, the book is the subject and the table belongs to the location phrase. Reversing those roles changes the claim even if the sentence still contains familiar words.

Check the copula separately from the location phrase. मैं कमरे में हूँ uses हूँ because the subject is मैं, not because the nearby noun कमरे has a particular ending. Likewise, आप कमरे में हैं retains the respectful agreement associated with आप. Learn a noun's grammatical gender with a useful example rather than guessing it from the object it names. When a changing adjective describes किताब, अच्छी is appropriate in यह किताब अच्छी है. Read a final response for both requested information and grammar: an accurate sentence about a different location does not answer the task. These are controlled standard examples; fuller treatment of tense, case and regional usage requires additional lessons.

## Keeping habitual aspect, agreement and place separate

In a simple habitual Hindi statement, the subject, habitual verb form and present auxiliary work together. With a feminine singular subject, a regular habitual form such as पढ़ती agrees accordingly; the auxiliary है marks the third-person singular present here. The place phrase पुस्तकालय में expresses location and does not determine the verb agreement. Do not translate an English word order mechanically: postpositions follow their noun phrase. A habitual statement says what normally happens, while a progressive statement describes an action in progress. The examples keep these meanings separate and do not attempt a complete account of Hindi tense, aspect or honorific agreement.

## Match the verb and locate the postposition

Simple Hindi statements often place the verb at the end, but agreement and postpositions carry relationships that word order alone cannot express. A habitual statement with a masculine singular subject commonly uses -ता है, while a feminine singular subject commonly uses -ती है. A masculine plural subject commonly uses -ते हैं. A noun before a postposition may take an oblique form: लड़का becomes लड़के before के पास. Possession expressed with के पास is a useful separate pattern; do not translate every English 'have' construction by inserting a verb in the English position.

## Worked example 1

Translate The book is on the table using किताब and मेज़.

1. Choose किताब as the subject.

2. Place the location postposition after the location noun: मेज़ पर.

3. Finish the singular description with है.

Answer: किताब मेज़ पर है।

## Worked example 2

Explain the difference between कमरा and कमरे in कमरा बड़ा है and मैं कमरे में हूँ.

1. In the first sentence कमरा is the subject noun: the room is big.

2. In the second sentence the noun appears before the postposition में.

3. The form कमरे is oblique singular here; it refers to a room, not automatically to multiple rooms.

Answer: कमरा is the direct form; कमरे is the oblique singular form in कमरे में.

## Worked example 3

Change मैं कमरे में हूँ to address someone respectfully, keeping the same location.

1. Replace the first-person subject मैं with respectful आप.

2. Keep the location phrase कमरे में unchanged.

3. Change हूँ to the copula हैं used with आप.

Answer: आप कमरे में हैं।

## Worked example 4

Translate “Sita studies in the library” as a simple habitual statement, using सीता, पुस्तकालय and पढ़ना.

1. Place में after पुस्तकालय to express in the library.

2. Use the feminine singular habitual form पढ़ती with सीता.

3. Use है as the present auxiliary for this third-person singular statement.

Answer: सीता पुस्तकालय में पढ़ती है।

## Misconceptions

- A Hindi postposition comes before its noun because an English preposition does.
  Correction: The postposition normally follows its noun phrase, as in मेज़ पर.

- आप takes है whenever it addresses only one person.
  Correction: Respectful आप normally uses हैं even for one person.

## Flashcards

### Complete: मैं घर में ___.
हूँ: मैं घर में हूँ।

### Complete a respectful description: आप शिक्षक ___.
हैं: आप शिक्षक हैं।

### What do में and पर express in basic location phrases?
में commonly expresses in; पर commonly expresses on or at, depending on context.

### Does कमरे in कमरे में necessarily refer to several rooms?
No. It is the oblique singular form of कमरा before में in this phrase.

### Why is अच्छी used in यह किताब अच्छी है?
किताब is grammatically feminine, and this adjective has a feminine form अच्छी.

### What determines हूँ in मैं कमरे में हूँ?
The subject मैं determines the copula; the nearby location noun does not control that agreement.

### Where does में occur in पुस्तकालय में?
After the noun phrase; it is a postposition expressing location here.

### What does पढ़ती है express in the Sita example?
A feminine singular habitual present statement, not by itself an action currently in progress.
